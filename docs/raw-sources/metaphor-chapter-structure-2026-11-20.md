## Overall structure and length

- Single-player RPG, Studio Zero / Sega; PC release 2024-10-11. Time runs on a day-night cycle, weather and a calendar;
  time-sensitive events exist and missing a story deadline is a game over. Travel spends time, which is spent on Royal
  Virtues, Followers, stat training or requests. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphor:_ReFantazio
- Main story length estimates range from about 45 to 100+ hours, most around 55 to 90 (80 hours most common); full
  completion about 150 hours. Estimates only. Source (search summary of several outlets, e.g. Shacknews, GamesRadar,
  Game8): https://shacknews.com/article/141760/how-long-is-metaphor-refantazio
- An official "chapter" or "act" list with per-chapter hours was NOT found. A community walkthrough splits the game by
  location: Prologue, Grand Trad, Martira, Port Brilehaven, Virga Island, Altabury Heights, Finale. Source:
  https://gamerguides.com/metaphor-refantazio/guide/walkthrough . Whether the game itself names these "chapters":
  [NEEDS VERIFICATION]. Per-part durations: [NEEDS VERIFICATION].
- The free Prologue Demo covers the opening: the first four dungeons, seven of the 40+ Archetypes and six Followers,
  about five hours. Demo saves carry into the full game, which skips the prologue. Sources:
  https://store.steampowered.com/app/3130330/Metaphor_ReFantazio__Prologue_Demo/ ,

## Order systems first appear (opening)

Sourced from the Gamer Guides prologue and Grand Trad pages:
https://www.gamerguides.com/metaphor-refantazio/guide/walkthrough/prologue and
https://www.gamerguides.com/metaphor-refantazio/guide/walkthrough/grand-trad (the second is a boss page; the walkthrough
tree under it is a guess at its place in the order).

1. Start: name entry, difficulty choice (Storyteller, Easy, Normal, Hard; Storyteller is locked in).
2. Field exploration (Tradia Desert): movement, dash, dodge tutorial; the fairy companion's Fae's Sight in the field;
   enemies are avoided, no formal battle described. Optional herb gathering.
3. First town (Grand Trad): shop (an item merchant), a questionnaire setting starting stats, naming, key item for magic.
   Dialogue and menu-heavy.
4. Travel by map to the next location (Northern Border Fort). Whether it is the first dungeon: [NEEDS VERIFICATION].
   Another guide calls the first dungeon the Grand Cathedral with the first boss there (via search summary of
   https://escapistmagazine.com/?p=285090 ) [NEEDS VERIFICATION], and the Grand Trad boss page covers the first
   scripted boss battle.
5. First battle: turn-based Press Turn combat, then action-style field combat ("Fast & Squad"); Synthesis skills unlock
   at the start of the first boss fight (boss page above).
6. Followers and Archetypes arrive together: after that boss the first Bond forms and unlocks the Knight Archetype;
   the fairy companion's bond rises. Archetype changing and the rank system follow. (boss page above)
7. Calendar: after the first boss the game advances to the next day and free time opens (boss page above).
8. Magla hollows (save and fast-travel rooms), Royal Virtues, Academy, fishing: timing NOT found.
   Magla hollows are described in https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphor:_ReFantazio . Fishing has its own guide page
   linked from the prologue page. All placed in the chapter-1 opening only as [NEEDS VERIFICATION].
