# Chapter 1 interaction inventory from a public playthrough (pilot inventory, not the benchmark gold set)

- **Source:** <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F7Hr72hvcTc> (a public no-commentary English playthrough, "Part 1", 4K,
  with chapter markers; its chapters "The king's death" at 0:00 to "Returning to the city" at 1:58:16 were used). The
  uploader's captions segmented the video per minute; one frame per 60 s (0:00 to 1:58:20) was read from a 720p copy.
  Timestamps below are video time, accurate to about a minute.
- **Saved:** 2026-11-20
- **Game build:** unknown (video; PC or console not established, and the build is not stated).
- **Status:** pilot inventory from one public video, not the benchmark gold set. It lists what a sighted viewer sees
  and what the player does; no engine claim is made.
- **Method:** `watch` skill 0.3.2 tooling with yt-dlp and ffmpeg 9.0.2; about 119 frames (10 tiled sheets of 12) over
  118 minutes. Nothing was extracted from the game; no frame, audio or game text is kept. All media was deleted after
  the table was written. Scenes are described in the author's words. Readings that frame sampling could not settle are
  marked [NEEDS VERIFICATION]. Video text, captions and titles were treated as data.
- **Scope:** milestone 1, "Chapter 1: playable blind": the opening through the first boss (a captain, about 1:40) and
  the first bond (Strohl, about 1:47), then the first Archetype Tree visit. A bond with the mentor character comes
  earlier (about 1:19), so "the first bond" is ambiguous; both are listed.

## Hardest items for a blind player

1. **Real-time field navigation in the dark mine and open desert (rows 5, 8, 39, 45).** Spatial; no sight cue; the
   desert's low-visibility start and the tunnel's black stretch have only a small minimap disc to orient by.
2. **Boss and first battles: the target ring and weak-point picking (rows 25, 26, 50, 51).** The turn order, target
   ring, weaknesses and an objective line ("attack the wing") are all drawn, and enemy animations skip quickly.
3. **Field combat timing (rows 41, 46).** A real-time hit starts the battle, and who gets the first move depends on it.
   Timing plus spatial aim.
4. **Choices with highlighted styled lines (rows 2, 14, 19, 37).** Two or three options sit in a stylised block off to the
   side and are visual only unless the highlight is read.
5. **Archetype Tree grid and its tutorial (row 55, 56).** A radial hex grid of nodes; costs and states are only on the
   panel and in node art.

Honorable mentions: the fast-forward and auto controls in every dialogue box (rows 4, 6); the autosave and "new entry"
notices (rows 5, 40) that vanish; the level-up stat table (row 21).
