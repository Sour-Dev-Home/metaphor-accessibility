- **Front and back, and height, in the navigation sounds (testing).** The sonar, wall tones and route pings are
  brighter, reaching up to about 12 kHz, each family in its own colour. `field.behindMuffle` makes those sounds and the
  guidance beep duller and a little quieter when they come from behind; `field.turnPings` plays the sonar's last ping
  and a recent route ping again when you turn the camera; `field.heightPan` moves a sound nearer the centre the farther
  above or below you it is. The angles are `field.behindMuffleDegrees` and `field.turnPingDegrees` in `limits`
  (#903, #839, #650). `[NEEDS VERIFICATION by ear]`
